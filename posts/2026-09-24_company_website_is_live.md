This past 2 weeks, I tried to update my company website. So it looks more
professional. Previous design was only my logo, company name, address, email, 
vat and company number.

![old website](/post/2026-09-24_company_website_is_live_1.png)

The new website looks better in my opinion, the logo has also changed.

![new website](/post/2026-09-24_company_website_is_live_2.png)

So I build it with Angular 22 and used TailwindCSS, the backend is just plain
express, but it is hosted on Netlify, so using express is a little bit
overkill, as it will be used as netlify-function.

The only interactive part is the contact page, you can send a message.
Writing the code, is done manually, no AI used. The logo and colors are also
done by my brother. The pictures are AI generated, the text are originally 
generated by AI, but then I changed it a little. So you can see the same 
kind of text on other company websites as well.

During development, I wanted to add extra pages like services, projects,...
but I didn't had content for those pages yet. And didn't wanted to generate
example page, so I removed them. I'll add them, back once I've decided
what to put inside those pages.

However, I did make it mobile-first.

![mobile version](/post/2026-09-24_company_website_is_live_3.png)

### It's a really basic website, why did it take 2 weeks?

I didn't worked hours per day on the site. When I started, that day I just
created the main stylelless version, added routing, think about what to
add, what to remove. Then looked several times to other company websites
to have an idea what to build. Started coding for 1 hour with a lot of
distraction. Then didn't looked at it for 3 days, then coded for another 
3 hours with distraction, ...

I'm also not a designer, so I do care that it should be nice, but I don't 
have a strong opinion as a UI/UX designer. So I also spend a lot of time
experimenting with different TailwindCSS classes.
